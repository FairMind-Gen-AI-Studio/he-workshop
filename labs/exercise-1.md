# Exercise 1: Your plateau

**Time:** 5 minutes · **Where:** on paper, on your own · **Sheet:** [`kit/exercise-1.md`](../kit/exercise-1.md)

**Goal:** name the one failure of your agent that you will carry through the
whole day.

## Steps

1. Print `kit/exercise-1.md`, or copy its three questions onto a blank sheet.
   Write on paper, not on screen: the sheet stays on your desk all day.
2. **Your agent's most recurrent failure.** Write the specific thing, not a
   category. "Sometimes it gets it wrong" is no use. "It declares an endpoint
   done when it mocked the response" is usable.
3. **Which symptom of the plateau it matches.** Pick one: it reports work as
   finished that is not, review has become the bottleneck, it duplicates
   instead of reusing, or name your own.
4. **What you did the last time it happened.** Be honest. "I fixed it by hand"
   is the most common answer and it is a perfectly good one: the day is about
   turning that manual fix into an artifact.

## Done when

You have three written answers, and the first one is concrete enough that a
colleague could recognise the failure from your sentence alone.
